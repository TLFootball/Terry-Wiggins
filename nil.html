About Terry

More Than a Player

Terry Wiggins arrived in Blacksburg as one of the most highly-decorated linebackers in the 2026 recruiting class — a consensus 4-star prospect out of Coatesville Area High School in Pennsylvania.

At 6′3″, 226 lbs., Terry brings rare size, athleticism, and football IQ to the linebacker position. His senior year production — 92 tackles, 14 TFLs, 4 sacks, 4 blocked field goals — spoke for itself. So did the national rankings: #8 linebacker in the country per 247Sports, #9 per Rivals, and an invite to the Navy All-American Bowl.

Off the field, Terry is building a brand rooted in authenticity, family, and the relentless work ethic that got him to Virginia Tech. His freshman year is the starting line — and the best is ahead.
